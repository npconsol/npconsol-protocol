# NPC Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

NPC starts from a simple idea: the quiet characters in the background deserve a
console of their own. The project aims to be a Solana home that is plain about what
it knows. Every public claim is tied to a record, and a record that is not yet
confirmed stays visibly empty instead of being filled with a guess.

The mission is to give holders and newcomers one dependable place to check an address,
read the market, and find official links the moment they exist.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation

Launch the site, the register and the address console. Publish the contract address
and official links as soon as they are stated.

### Phase 2: Visibility

Deepen the market views, add more chain-level context, and widen the reference material
for newcomers.

### Phase 3: Community

Open public channels, publish the source repository and document how the register is maintained.

### Phase 4: Expansion

Decide on further tools and partnerships once the earlier phases are in place.
